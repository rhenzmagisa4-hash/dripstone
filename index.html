Goals

To create a comprehensive set of standardized childcare recipes that consistently deliver nutritious, age-appropriate, and culturally inclusive meals, while maintaining food safety and cost-effectiveness.

Objectives

  1. Identify and incorporate nutritional guidelines that align with childcare health and safety regulations.
  2. Develop standardized recipe formats that ensure consistency in taste, portion size, and nutritional value.
  3. Test recipes for acceptability, safety and ease of preparation in childcare kitchen environments.
  4. Train childcare staff in proper preparation methods and portion control.
  5. Establish a monitoring and feedback system for continuous improvement of recipes.
