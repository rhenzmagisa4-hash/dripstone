The Dripstone Family

Supporting children to engage in Risky Play requires us, the adults, to walk the line between support and challenge.

We recognise the fundamental role our educators play in your child's life. We support our team to be the best they can be. We place an emphasis on individual and team wellbeing.

We offer above-award wages and excellent working conditions to attract and retain the best educators. We also encourage and provide extensive training and professional development opportunities because we know that those who know better, do better.

We believe in our team's ability to be the change they want to see in the world. This is why we strive to grow each member of our team towards great outcomes for themselves, the children and the broader community. The future is bright at Dripstone.

Dripstone Children's Centre provides a nurturing, nature-inspired environment where children learn, grow, and develop lifelong healthy habits.
